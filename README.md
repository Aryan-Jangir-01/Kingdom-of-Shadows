# ⚔️ Kingdom of Shadows: Break the Curse
**Theme 2: Cursed Kingdom** — *SAVISKAR 2026 AI Game Maker Challenge*

A level-based 2D Action-Adventure Platformer built with HTML5 Canvas, Vanilla JavaScript, and procedural audio synthesis.

## 🏰 Chapters & Level Progression

### 🏚️ Level 1: The Cursed Village
* **Objective:** Explore the misty ruins and find **3 Petrified Stone Villagers**.
* **Mechanic:** Approach each villager and press `[E]` to channel holy light, shattering the stone crust and returning them to life.
* **Exit:** Once all 3 villagers are liberated, the ancient iron gate unlocks at the end of the village.

### 🕯️ Level 2: The Haunted Catacombs
* **Objective:** Venture into pitch-black underground catacombs and ignite **2 Ancient Sun Altars**.
* **Mechanic:** Dynamic torchlight radius illuminates only your surroundings. Approach the Sun Altars and press `[E]` to ignite radiant beams of sunlight.
* **Exit:** Reigniting both altars unlocks the passage to the King's Citadel.

### 👑 Level 3: The Dark Citadel (Boss Clash)
* **Objective:** Face **Malakor, The Cursed King** in his corrupted throne room.
* **Boss AI Phases:**
  * **Phase 1 (100% - 65% HP):** King swings giant shadow broadsword and casts homing purple curse skulls.
  * **Phase 2 (65% - 35% HP):** King slams the ground, unleashing ground shockwaves across the floor.
  * **Phase 3 (< 35% HP):** King raises an impenetrable **Curse Barrier**. 3 **Holy Light Spheres** manifest across the arena platforms. Collect all 3 to shatter his shield, deliver the final cleansing strike, and restore the kingdom!

## 💎 Features
- **Zero External Dependencies:** Built with pure HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript.
- **Procedural Audio (Web Audio API):** Synthesized jump, sword slash, enemy impacts, holy chime, and boss roar sounds with zero external audio assets.
- **Level Select Screen:** Easily jump directly to Level 1, 2, or 3 from the main menu for quick demonstrations and judging.
- **Full Touch & Mouse Support:** On-screen controls for mobile or seamless presentation on interactive screens.
